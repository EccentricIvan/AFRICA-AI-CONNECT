Why a .zip? Android Chrome silently blocks .apk downloads as a risky file type. The .zip always downloads — open it with your file manager and tap the .apk inside.
  1. Download the .zip above and open it in your Files app.
  2. Tap the .apk inside to extract and install it.
  3. Android will warn about an unknown source — this app isn't on the Play Store yet, so allow it for your browser or Files app.
  4. Open AI Connect Africa and choose your language.
